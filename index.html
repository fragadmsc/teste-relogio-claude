Questions

Before you decide.

I

Is every Fraga watch authentic, and how can I prove it?

+

Every piece is assembled in our workshop and ships with a numbered certificate of authenticity, a serial engraved on the case back and a sealed service booklet. Your watch is registered to you from day one.

II

Is it a safe investment, and will it hold its value?

+

We never promise returns, but steel-and-gold cases, limited runs and hand finishing are what collectors look for. Keep the box, papers and service record and your watch will remain easy to resell.

III

What if it doesn't suit me once it arrives?

+

You have 30 days to return it, fully insured, for a full refund. Wear it, see how it sits on your wrist, and decide without pressure.

IV

How is the watch serviced and protected over the years?

+

A 5-year warranty covers the movement. We recommend a service every five to seven years, performed by our own watchmakers with genuine parts, and we repair every Fraga for life.
